6. Results expectation
Do not expect all models to give high accuracy or high F1.
For low-resource languages, the expected research outcome is:
Some language pairs will perform well. 
Some target languages will perform poorly. 
Few-shot learning should usually improve over zero-shot. 
Related languages should generally transfer better. 
Indic-specific models may perform better in some cases, but not always. 
Token-level tasks such as NER may remain challenging. 
Sentence-level classification may perform better than token-level tagging. 
Your team must explain why results differ.
You should analyse:
Which model performs best? 
Which source language transfers best? 
Which target language is most difficult? 
Which task is easiest or hardest? 
Does few-shot help consistently? 
Does linguistic relatedness affect performance? 
Does script similarity affect performance? 
Which entity types or labels are most frequently confused?
